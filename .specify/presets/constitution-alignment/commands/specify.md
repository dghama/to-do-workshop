## Repository and Constitution alignment

Before drafting the specification, inspect the repository documentation and the
relevant existing modules, contracts, and quality guidance. If
`.specify/memory/constitution.md` exists, treat it as authoritative; do not
weaken it to fit technical debt or make unsupported assumptions about the
repository.

Capture user-visible behavior and testable acceptance criteria, and complete
the appended Quality and Risk Considerations section only for relevant areas.
Keep implementation choices in the plan.
