## Resolve material ambiguity

Use the standard feature-context resolution and read the complete resolved
specification before asking questions. Focus on ambiguities, contradictions,
missing outcomes, acceptance criteria, boundaries, or failure behavior that
could change the plan or implementation.

Before asking, inspect relevant source material named in the specification and
repository documentation that is available. Do not ask for information already
present in those sources or in a previously confirmed decision. Use Jira or
other external information only when it is actually available in the
conversation or through an installed integration; otherwise state that it was
not inspected rather than inferring its contents.

Ask only the highest-impact, answerable questions allowed by the standard
Clarify workflow. Apply confirmed answers to the relevant specification
sections, retain unanswered items as explicit open questions, and do not
silently convert assumptions into requirements or reopen confirmed decisions
without conflicting evidence. Distinguish product decisions, which require
stakeholder answers, from technical uncertainties, which belong in
repository-grounded planning unless they change the required outcome.

For each unresolved question, assess whether it blocks planning because its
answer could change user-visible behavior, scope, or acceptance criteria.
Report blocking and non-blocking open questions separately. Do not represent a
feature as ready for planning while a blocking product decision remains
unresolved, and do not claim approval that the user did not provide.
